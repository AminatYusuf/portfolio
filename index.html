About me

Creative, curious, and focused on clarity.

I am Yusuf Aminat Arike, an aspiring Logo, Brand, and Product Designer based in Lagos, Nigeria. I create visual concepts that help businesses and digital products communicate with confidence.

My work combines visual identity, promotional design, and user-focused product thinking. I enjoy taking an idea from a rough concept to a clear, useful, and visually consistent experience.

Currently developing ExplainIt, an educational mobile application built with MIT App Inventor.
